# Comprehensive Mutual Fund Instruction File

## Purpose
This document consolidates all user instructions, queries, and prompts regarding mutual fund investments in India. It is designed to be used for generating accurate, data-backed mutual fund portfolio recommendations in the future.

## User Objective
- Maximize returns through **high-performing mutual funds**.
- Ensure **reliable and accurate data** using official AMC websites, SEBI, and credible aggregator/research portals.
- Receive recommendations that consider **fund portfolio overlap ≤20%**.
- Generate **accurate SIP XIRR calculations** (1Y, 3Y, 5Y) for given SIP amounts.
- Analyze and adjust portfolio every **6 months**.

## Investment Strategy
- **Total SIP:** ₹50,000 per month.
- **SIP Dates:** 5th, 15th, 25th of each month.
- **SIP Allocation:** Provide allocation percentages per date for each fund.
- **Investment Horizon:** Minimum 3+ years.

## Portfolio Requirements
- Include **8 or more funds** covering all major categories:
  - Large Cap
  - Mid Cap
  - Small Cap
  - Flexi / Multi Cap
  - Large & Mid Cap
  - International / Global Equity
- Include **at least:**
  - 2 Small Cap funds
  - 2 Mid Cap funds
  - 1 Large & Mid Cap
  - 1 Large Cap
  - 1 Flexi / Multi Cap
  - 1 International / Global Equity
- Ensure a **balanced mix** of Aggressive, Growth, and Stable funds.
- **Target XIRR:** ≥15% per year over 1-, 3-, and 5-year periods.
- Avoid fund duplication; **ensure fund portfolio overlap ≤20%**.

## Data & Fund Selection Criteria
- Use **real, existing funds only**.
- Consider **historical SIP XIRR performance**, **fund ratings**, **fund house credibility**, and **long-term consistency**.
- Avoid hypothetical or generic suggestions.
- Ensure funds provide **sectoral and geographic diversification**.

## Output Format

## Review & Rebalance Strategy
- Analyze portfolio **every 6 months**.
- Suggest **trim/add funds** if required based on updated performance and overlap.
- Adjust allocation percentages according to **market trends and fund performance**.

## Analysis Approach

## Advanced Fund Selection Filters (Mandatory)
When selecting, ranking, or recommending mutual funds, apply the following additional quantitative filters:

### Risk-Adjusted Performance Criteria
- Alpha should be between 6 and 15 and remain reasonably consistent across 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year periods.
- Sharpe Ratio should be greater than 1.0.
- Beta should be between 0.9 and 1.2.
- Down-Market Alpha should be positive, indicating resilience during market corrections.
- Return vs Benchmark: The fund must consistently outperform its benchmark across multiple time periods.

### Benchmark Mapping
Use the following benchmark indices for performance comparison:
- Small Cap → Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI
- Mid Cap → Nifty Midcap 150 TRI
- Large Cap → Nifty 100 TRI
- Flexi Cap → Nifty 500 TRI

### Approved Data Sources
Fund analysis, validation, and comparison data should be sourced from:
- Morningstar India
- Value Research
- Moneycontrol
- AMFI
- Powerup

### Fund Selection Rule
Preference should be given to funds that satisfy all of the above criteria. If a fund fails any criterion, clearly explain the reason and provide an alternative fund that meets the requirements.


## Enhanced Professional Screening Framework (Mandatory)

### Sortino Ratio
- Prefer funds with Sortino Ratio > 1.2.
- Higher Sortino Ratio is preferred over Sharpe Ratio when comparing funds with similar returns.
- Avoid funds with weak downside-risk-adjusted performance.

### Information Ratio
- Prefer Information Ratio > 0.5.
- Strong preference for funds with Information Ratio > 0.75 over 3-year and 5-year periods.
- Indicates consistency of excess returns over benchmark.

### Rolling Return Consistency
- Evaluate 3-Year and 5-Year Rolling Returns.
- Fund should outperform category average and benchmark in at least 70% of rolling return periods.
- Avoid funds with highly volatile rolling-return patterns.

### Fund Manager Evaluation
- Fund Manager tenure should preferably be at least 5 years.
- Avoid funds with frequent fund manager changes.
- Give additional weight to funds managed successfully across multiple market cycles.

### Assets Under Management (AUM)
- Large Cap/Flexi Cap: Prefer AUM above ₹5,000 Crore.
- Mid Cap: Prefer AUM above ₹3,000 Crore.
- Small Cap: Prefer AUM above ₹2,000 Crore.
- Avoid extremely small funds unless they demonstrate exceptional performance and stability.

### Expense Ratio Filters
- Direct Plans only.
- Large Cap/Flexi Cap: Prefer Expense Ratio below 1.0%.
- Mid Cap/Small Cap: Prefer Expense Ratio below 1.25%.
- Lower expense ratios receive higher ranking when performance is similar.

### Portfolio Overlap Validation
- Maximum overlap between any two recommended funds: 20%.
- If overlap exceeds 20%, recommend only the stronger fund unless there is a compelling diversification reason.
- Explicitly report overlap percentages in portfolio reviews.

### Risk Management Rules
- Avoid funds with persistent underperformance for 3 consecutive years.
- Avoid funds with significantly negative Alpha.
- Avoid funds with Beta greater than 1.3 unless specifically justified.
- Prefer funds demonstrating resilience during market corrections.

### Portfolio Review Scorecard
Rank each fund using:
1. Alpha
2. Sharpe Ratio
3. Sortino Ratio
4. Information Ratio
5. Rolling Return Consistency
6. Down-Market Alpha
7. Expense Ratio
8. Fund Manager Stability
9. Benchmark Outperformance
10. Portfolio Overlap Impact

### SEBI-Compliant Advisory Framework
- Recommendations should be educational and data-driven.
- Clearly disclose risks.
- Avoid return guarantees.
- Use historical data only as an indicator and not as assurance of future performance.
- Prioritize suitability, diversification, and risk-adjusted returns.
