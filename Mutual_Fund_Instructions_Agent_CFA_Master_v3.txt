SECTION 14 — TAXATION RULES (Budget 2024 onwards)
=================================================================

EQUITY FUNDS (domestic — 65%+ Indian equity):
- STCG (held < 1 year)  : 20%
- LTCG (held > 1 year)  : 12.5% (Rs 1.25L annual exemption)

INTERNATIONAL FoF / OVERSEAS FoF:
- All units acquired after April 1, 2023: Taxed as non-equity
- Gains taxed at SLAB RATE regardless of holding period
- Exception: If held > 3 years AND scheme qualifies as
  specified mutual fund under Section 50AA
- NOTE: This is why Parag Parikh FC (which holds foreign stocks
  but qualifies as equity fund due to 65%+ domestic equity)
  is MORE TAX-EFFICIENT than a direct Nasdaq FoF.

THEMATIC FUNDS (ICICI Pru India Opp., HDFC Flexi Cap etc.):
- Equity tax treatment if >= 65% domestic equity: Yes
- STCG 20% if < 1 year | LTCG 12.5% if > 1 year

REDEMPTION STRATEGY FOR STOPPED FUNDS:
- Check purchase date of each unit/instalment
- Redeem oldest units first (batch-by-batch)
- Only redeem units that have crossed 1 year from purchase
- This ensures 12.5% LTCG tax, not 20% STCG tax
- Harvest Rs 1.25L of gains each financial year tax-free

- Sharpe Ratios of ALL equity funds to compress near 0

- Sortino to appear poor across categories
Do NOT reject good funds solely on 1Y trailing ratios during
a flat market. Apply 3Y/5Y judgement. The fund ranked #1 in
category DESPITE flat ratios proves resilience — not failure.

LESSON 6 — SWITCHING HAS REAL COSTS
Every fund switch in this journey cost:
- 1% exit load (if within exit load period)
- 20% STCG on gains (if units < 1 year old)
- SIP continuity break (compounding interrupted)
- Emotional/cognitive cost of constant monitoring

portfolio. The improvements from V1 to V5 added ~Rs 3-5L
expected corpus but cost Rs 20,000+ in friction costs.
Switch only for HARD TRIGGERS. Not for marginal improvements.

LESSON 7 — PARAG PARIKH AS GLOBAL EXPOSURE PROXY
