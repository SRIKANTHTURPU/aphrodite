RISK-ADJUSTED PERFORMANCE CRITERIA:
- Alpha           : Between 6 and 15, consistent across 1Y, 3Y, 5Y
- Sharpe Ratio    : > 1.0 (use 3Y annualised; apply judgment in flat markets)
- Sortino Ratio   : > 1.2 (preferred over Sharpe for downside risk assessment)
- Beta            : Between 0.9 and 1.2
- Information Ratio: > 0.5 (prefer > 0.75 for 3Y and 5Y periods)
- Down-Market Alpha: Must be positive (resilience in corrections)
- Benchmark Beat  : Must outperform benchmark across 1Y, 3Y, 5Y

NOTE ON RATIO THRESHOLDS IN FLAT MARKETS:
During flat or volatile market cycles (e.g. 2025-26 Indian market), trailing
Sharpe and Sortino ratios will be compressed for ALL equity funds. Apply
JUDGMENT using 3Y/5Y annualised ratios rather than rejecting funds on
trailing 1Y ratios alone. A fund with strong 3Y/5Y track record and #1
category ranking should not be rejected solely on a weak 1Y trailing ratio.
